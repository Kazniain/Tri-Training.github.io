New training plan

Generates a personalised week-by-week plan from established, publicly documented endurance-training principles (progressive overload, a base/build/race-specific/taper structure, and a recovery week roughly every fourth week). It is a structured estimate, not a certified coaching plan or medical advice — see the note at the bottom before you rely on it, especially for longer distances or any pre-existing health conditions.

Athlete profile

Resting-energy estimates use the Mifflin-St Jeor equation (a published, widely used formula); exercise-calorie estimates use standard MET activity values. The training plan structure itself is this app's own rules-based implementation of common periodisation principles, not a licensed program from a certified coaching body. If you have any underlying health conditions, are new to endurance training, or are training for 70.3/full distance, please have the plan reviewed by a coach or doctor before following it.
